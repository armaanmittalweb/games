Privacy

Game Night has no accounts, no ads and no ad trackers, and sells nothing about you. To play, you only type a name. A room, with its chat and drawings, deletes itself a day after it was last used; beyond that the site keeps random ids, counts, and any feedback you choose to send.

Last updated 7 October 2026.

In short

  • No sign-up. We never ask for your email, phone number, age or real name. The name you play under can be anything.
  • Rooms are temporary. A room, with everything said and drawn in it, is deleted 24 hours after it was last used.
  • Counts are anonymous. We count visits and games with random ids made by your browser, never with names.
  • No cookies from us. Game Night stores a few small values in your browser instead (listed below), which you can clear at any time.

What is kept in your browser

These live in your browser's storage for games.amittal.dev. Clearing the site's data removes all of them.

What a room keeps

Each room is held on Cloudflare's servers while it is in use. It keeps:

  • the name and colour of everyone in it, their random player id, and when they joined;
  • the games played: moves, answers, drawings, scores and the room's leaderboard;
  • the room chat (the last 80 messages).

Everyone in the room can see your name, your chat messages and whatever the game shows them, such as your drawings and answers. A room and everything in it is deleted automatically 24 hours after it was last used, once nobody is connected to it. Room codes are reused after that, but nothing from the old room carries over.

The anonymous counts

To know whether the games work and what people enjoy, the site counts:

  • Page views: the page, the website you came from (its domain only, such as google.com), a utm_source tag if the link had one, your country (two letters, worked out by Cloudflare from your connection; your IP address is not stored), whether you are on a phone, tablet or computer, the time, and your browser and visit ids.
  • Rooms and games: a random room id and its code, which games were played, how many players, when they started and ended, how many moves and errors there were, and whether dropped connections came back. Players are recorded by their random ids.
  • Who is in a room right now, by browser id, for a live count.
  • Taps on Share (whether by your phone's share sheet, copying the link or the QR code) and script errors (the first 120 characters of the error message).
  • Feedback you choose to send after a game: the stars, whether you would play again, and up to 300 characters of text. Please do not put personal details in it.

Totals per room and per player id are deleted after 30 days. The anonymous records above are kept so trends can be compared over time; they hold no names, email addresses or IP addresses.

Pages on the site also send a separate page-view count to api.amittal.dev, the dashboard for all of Armaan Mittal's sites: the site name, the page and the page you came from. It carries no ids.

Other services involved

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and runs the rooms. Like any web host it handles your IP address to deliver the pages. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Apple: Music Guess plays 30-second song previews straight from Apple's servers, so Apple receives that request (including your IP address) when a clip plays. The "Listen on Apple Music" link opens Apple's site. See Apple's privacy policy.
  • Sharing a room uses your own device's share sheet or clipboard, and the QR code is drawn in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere until you choose where to share it.

There are no advertising, social media or third-party analytics scripts on Game Night.

Leaving the counts

Open games.amittal.dev/?me=1 once in a browser and everything that browser does is marked and left out of every count from then on. Clearing the site's data from your browser removes your ids; a new random id is made next time you visit.

Questions or deletion

Game Night is made by Armaan Mittal in India. To ask what is kept about a room or about your browser's id (you will find it in your browser's site data as gn.vid), or to have it deleted, use the contact form. Messages sent through it, with any way to reply you leave, are kept for 30 days.
