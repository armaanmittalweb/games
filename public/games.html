All 22 games

Free multiplayer games for friends, in the browser. Make one room and switch between games all night; the room keeps score across every game.

Word

  • Code WordsTwo teams. Spymasters give one-word clues to lead teammates to their agents.4 to 16 players · about 12 min
  • Word RaceMultiplayer Wordle: the same five-letter words for everyone, one timer.1 to 30 players · about 7 min
  • Dictionary BluffInvent a definition for a strange real word. Fool your friends, find the real one.3 to 12 players · about 5 min
  • Word GridA grid of letters, a timer. Find words by chaining neighbouring letters.1 to 20 players · about 3 min

Drawing

  • Draw & GuessOne player draws, everyone else races to guess the word.3 to 16 players · about 7 min
  • TelephoneWrite a phrase, draw what you read, describe what you see. Watch it fall apart.3 to 12 players · about 10 min

Party

  • Mind Meld"Name a fruit." Score by giving the same answer as everyone else.3 to 20 players · about 4 min
  • Most Likely ToWho is most likely to… Everyone points at once. Score by agreeing with the room.3 to 20 players · about 3 min

Deception

  • ImposterEveryone gets the same word except one. Give clues, then find the imposter.3 to 16 players · about 5 min

Trivia

  • TriviaQuick-fire multiple choice. Right and fast beats right and slow.1 to 30 players · about 4 min
  • Closest WinsHow tall is the Burj Khalifa? Nobody knows. Closest guess wins.2 to 30 players · about 4 min
  • GeoGuessA country or city is named. Drop your pin on the world map. Closest wins.1 to 30 players · about 5 min
  • Music GuessTwo seconds of a Bollywood song. Name it before your friends do.1 to 30 players · about 4 min
  • Movie GuessName the film from emojis. A story and a famous line follow if you need them.1 to 30 players · about 5 min

Puzzle

  • ConnectionsSixteen words, four hidden groups. Find them before you make four mistakes.1 to 30 players · about 4 min
  • MastermindCrack the secret colour code in as few guesses as you can. Play alone, or race your friends on the same code.1 to 6 players · about 6 min
  • 24 GameFour numbers. Add, subtract, multiply and divide to make 24. First to get it wins most.1 to 30 players · about 4 min

Strategy

  • TerritoryGrab squares on a shared map. Everyone moves at once, and clashes cancel out.2 to 8 players · about 8 min
  • AuctionEveryone gets 100 coins. Bid on lots, collect sets, and do not spend it all.2 to 10 players · about 7 min

Cards

  • Last CardMatch the colour or number, play action cards, and do not forget to call last card.2 to 10 players · about 9 min

Reflex

  • ReactionWait for green. Tap. Fastest finger wins, false starts lose.1 to 30 players · about 2 min
  • Stop the ClockStart a hidden stopwatch and stop it at exactly 7.30 seconds, by feel. Closest wins.1 to 30 players · about 2 min
