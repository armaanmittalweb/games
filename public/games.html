All 15 games

Free multiplayer games for friends, in the browser. Make one room and switch between games all night; the room keeps score across every game.

Drawing

Draw & Guess

One player draws, everyone else races to guess the word.

3 to 16 players · about 7 min

Telephone

Write a phrase, draw what you read, describe what you see. Watch it fall apart.

3 to 12 players · about 10 min

Deception

Imposter

Everyone gets the same word except one. Give clues, then find the imposter.

3 to 16 players · about 5 min

Word

Code Words

Two teams. Spymasters give one-word clues to lead teammates to their agents.

4 to 16 players · about 12 min

Word Race

Multiplayer Wordle: the same five-letter words for everyone, one timer.

1 to 30 players · about 7 min

Dictionary Bluff

Invent a definition for a strange real word. Fool your friends, find the real one.

3 to 12 players · about 5 min

Word Grid

A grid of letters, a timer. Find words by chaining neighbouring letters.

1 to 20 players · about 3 min

Party

Mind Meld

"Name a fruit." Score by giving the same answer as everyone else.

3 to 20 players · about 4 min

Most Likely To

Who is most likely to… Everyone points at once. Score by agreeing with the room.

3 to 20 players · about 3 min

Trivia

Trivia

Quick-fire multiple choice. Right and fast beats right and slow.

1 to 30 players · about 4 min

Closest Wins

How tall is the Burj Khalifa? Nobody knows. Closest guess wins.

2 to 30 players · about 4 min

Cards & dice

Last Card

Match the colour or number, play action cards, and do not forget to call last card.

2 to 10 players · about 9 min

Liar's Dice

Hidden dice, rising bids, and one word: liar.

2 to 8 players · about 8 min

Reflex

Reaction

Wait for green. Tap. Fastest finger wins, false starts lose.

1 to 30 players · about 2 min

Stop the Clock

Start a hidden stopwatch and stop it at exactly 7.30 seconds, by feel. Closest wins.

1 to 30 players · about 2 min
