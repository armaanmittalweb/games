How to play Most Likely To

  1. Make a room. Type your name, tap Create a room and send the five-character code (or the link) to your friends. Phones and laptops can share a room.
  2. A prompt appears: "Who is most likely to survive a zombie apocalypse?" Everyone secretly votes for a player.
  3. The votes are revealed together, and the most-voted player takes the title.
  4. You score 2 points when you voted for the player who got the most votes.

Settings

The host can change these in the room before starting:

  • Rounds: 3 to 20 (default 8).
  • Seconds to vote: 10 to 60 seconds (default 20 seconds).
