Telephone Drawing Game Online (Like Gartic Phone)

Telephone is a telephone drawing game in the style of Gartic Phone you can play free in your browser. Write a phrase, draw what you read, describe what you see. Watch it fall apart.

How to play Telephone

  1. Make a room. Type your name, tap Create a room and send the five-character code (or the link) to your friends. Phones and laptops can share a room.
  2. Everyone writes a phrase. Each phrase starts its own book.
  3. Books pass around the room: you draw the phrase you are given, the next player sees only your drawing and writes what it shows, the next draws that, and so on.
  4. At the end the books are revealed step by step. Give a like to the best moments; likes are the score.

Settings

The host can change these in the room before starting:

  • Seconds to write: 15 to 120 seconds (default 40 seconds).
  • Seconds to draw: 20 to 180 seconds (default 75 seconds).
