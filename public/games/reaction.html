Reaction Time Test Game Multiplayer with Friends

Reaction is a multiplayer reaction time game you can play free in your browser. Wait for green. Tap. Fastest finger wins, false starts lose.

How to play Reaction

  1. Make a room. Type your name, tap Create a room and send the five-character code (or the link) to your friends. Phones and laptops can share a room.
  2. Each round, wait for the signal and tap as fast as you can. Tap too early and it is a false start: no points that round.
  3. Rounds vary: plain wait-for-green, decoy colours that must not be tapped, and a target that appears somewhere on the screen.
  4. The fastest player each round gets 10 points, then 7, 5, 4, 3, 2 and 1.

Settings

The host can change these in the room before starting:

  • Rounds: 3 to 15 (default 6).
