24 Game Online: Make 24 with Friends

24 Game is the 24 maths puzzle you can play free in your browser. Four numbers. Add, subtract, multiply and divide to make 24. First to get it wins most.

How to play 24 Game

  1. Make a room. Type your name, tap Create a room and send the five-character code (or the link) to your friends. Phones and laptops can share a room.
  2. Four numbers from 1 to 13. Use each one exactly once, with + − × and ÷, to make 24.
  3. Tap a number, an operation and another number to combine them; keep going until one number is left. Undo any time.
  4. Every hand can be solved. First to 24 scores 10, then 7, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1. Hard hands need a fraction along the way.

Settings

The host can change these in the room before starting:

  • Hands: 3 to 20 (default 8).
  • Seconds per hand: 20 to 180 seconds (default 60 seconds).
  • Difficulty: Easy and medium, Easy, Medium, Hard (default Easy and medium).
