Estimation Game Online: Closest Guess Wins

Closest Wins is an estimation quiz where the closest guess wins you can play free in your browser. How tall is the Burj Khalifa? Nobody knows. Closest guess wins.

How to play Closest Wins

  1. Make a room. Type your name, tap Create a room and send the five-character code (or the link) to your friends. Phones and laptops can share a room.
  2. A question with a number for an answer: a height, a year, a distance, a count. Everyone types a guess.
  3. The closest guess scores 10 points, then 7, 5, 4, 3, 2 and 1. For big numbers, closeness is measured by ratio, so being off by half is the same at any size.

Settings

The host can change these in the room before starting:

  • Rounds: 3 to 20 (default 8).
  • Seconds to answer: 10 to 90 seconds (default 30 seconds).
