Uno-Style Card Game Online with Friends, Free

Last Card is a colour-matching card game in the style of Uno you can play free in your browser. Match the colour or number, play action cards, and do not forget to call last card.

How to play Last Card

  1. Make a room. Type your name, tap Create a room and send the five-character code (or the link) to your friends. Phones and laptops can share a room.
  2. Play a card that matches the top card by colour, number or symbol. Wild cards go on anything and you choose the next colour.
  3. Skip misses the next player, Reverse changes direction, +2 and Wild +4 make the next player draw. With stacking on, they can pass it on with a +2 or +4 of their own.
  4. Cannot play? Draw one. If it fits, you can play it straight away.
  5. Down to one card? Tap Last card before anyone catches you, or you draw two.
  6. First to empty their hand wins; the rest are ranked by the fewest cards left.

Settings

The host can change these in the room before starting:

  • Cards each: 4 to 10 (default 7).
  • Stack +2 and +4: Yes, No (default Yes).
  • Seconds per turn: 10 to 90 seconds (default 30 seconds).
