Codenames-Style Word Game Online for Teams

Code Words is a team word-association game in the style of Codenames you can play free in your browser. Two teams. Spymasters give one-word clues to lead teammates to their agents.

How to play Code Words

  1. Make a room. Type your name, tap Create a room and send the five-character code (or the link) to your friends. Phones and laptops can share a room.
  2. Players split into Red and Blue, and each team picks a spymaster. The spymasters see which of the 25 words belong to which team.
  3. On their turn a spymaster gives a one-word clue and a number: how many words it points to.
  4. Teammates tap words to reveal them. A right guess lets them keep going (up to the number plus one). A neutral word or the other team's word ends the turn.
  5. First team to find all its agents wins. Reveal a black card (the assassin) and your team loses at once. The host can add up to 4 black cards for a riskier board.
