Territory Game Online: Claim the Map with Friends

Territory is a simultaneous territory-claiming strategy game you can play free in your browser. Grab squares on a shared map. Everyone moves at once, and clashes cancel out.

How to play Territory

  1. Make a room. Type your name, tap Create a room and send the five-character code (or the link) to your friends. Phones and laptops can share a room.
  2. Everyone starts with one square. Each turn, secretly pick one free square next to your land.
  3. All picks are shown at once. If two players pick the same square, nobody gets it.
  4. Wall off a free area so that only your land touches it, and the whole area becomes yours. Rocks block the way.
  5. The game ends when nobody can grow. Most squares wins.

Settings

The host can change these in the room before starting:

  • Seconds per turn: 5 to 30 seconds (default 12 seconds).
  • Map: Small, Normal, Big (default Normal).
