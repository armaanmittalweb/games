How to play Trivia

  1. Make a room. Type your name, tap Create a room and send the five-character code (or the link) to your friends. Phones and laptops can share a room.
  2. Everyone gets the same question with four choices (or true or false) and a timer.
  3. A right answer scores 500 points plus up to 500 more for speed. Three in a row and you get a streak bonus.
  4. The questions are about India unless the host picks another topic in the settings. India questions are written for this game; the other topics come from the Open Trivia Database.
