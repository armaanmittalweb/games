Mind Meld: Think Alike Party Game Online

Mind Meld is a think-alike party game you can play free in your browser. "Name a fruit." Score by giving the same answer as everyone else.

How to play Mind Meld

  1. Make a room. Type your name, tap Create a room and send the five-character code (or the link) to your friends. Phones and laptops can share a room.
  2. Everyone answers the same prompt at once, like "Name a fruit".
  3. You score one point for every other player who gave the same answer. Small spelling differences and plurals still match.
  4. The goal is not to be clever. It is to think like the group.

Settings

The host can change these in the room before starting:

  • Rounds: 3 to 20 (default 8).
  • Seconds to answer: 10 to 60 seconds (default 25 seconds).
