Draw and Guess Online with Friends, Free

Draw & Guess is a Pictionary-style drawing and guessing game you can play free in your browser. One player draws, everyone else races to guess the word.

How to play Draw & Guess

  1. Make a room. Type your name, tap Create a room and send the five-character code (or the link) to your friends. Phones and laptops can share a room.
  2. Each turn one player picks one of three words and draws it. Everyone else types guesses.
  3. Letters of the word are revealed as hints as time runs down. A guess one letter off is marked "close".
  4. Guess sooner to score more. The drawer scores for every player who gets it.
  5. Once you have guessed, your messages are only shown to others who have guessed too.
