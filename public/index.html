How it works

  1. Make a roomType your name and tap Create a room. You get a five-character code.
  2. Invite friendsSend the link or read out the code. Up to 30 people can join, sitting together or on a call.
  3. Pick a game, or plan a nightPlay any game, or let the site line up a game night for your group. Every game adds to one room table.

Questions

How do I play games with friends online here?

Type your name and tap Create a room. Send the five-character code or the link to your friends. When everyone is in, the host picks a game, or plans a whole game night, and starts.

Is it free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no download, no ads and no limit on games.

How many people can play?

Up to 30 people can be in a room. Each game has its own range: Code Words needs 4, Telephone and Imposter need 3, Word Race, Trivia and Reaction work even alone.

What is a game night?

The host picks a length (Quick, Standard, Chaos, Tournament or Endless) and a mood, and the site lines up games that fit the group. Every game adds room points to one table for the night. In a Tournament the bottom players are knocked out after each game until two play the final.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. Every game is built for touch screens as well as keyboards, and phones and laptops can share one room. A dropped connection or a refresh puts you back where you were.

Do we need to be on a call?

No, but it is more fun. Every game works with the room chat, and Imposter, Code Words and Draw & Guess are even better when you can hear each other.
