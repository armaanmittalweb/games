Terms of use

Game Night is a free website of party games. Using it means you agree to these terms. They are short and in plain English.

Last updated 10 October 2026.

Free, with no account

Game Night costs nothing and needs no sign-up. You play under any name you type. There are no ads and nothing to buy.

Play nice

You are responsible for what you put into a room: your name, chat, drawings, answers and anything else you type. Do not use Game Night to:

  • abuse, threaten, bully or harass anyone, or attack people for their religion, caste, gender, sexuality, region or anything else they are;
  • share sexual content involving anyone under 18, or any content that is illegal in India;
  • share someone else's phone number, address, photos or other personal details without their permission;
  • flood rooms, cheat with scripts, or try to break or overload the site.

The host of a room can remove anyone from it. If someone breaks these rules, report it.

Rooms are open to anyone with the code

Anyone who has a room's code or link can join it, so share it only with people you want to play with. A room and everything said and drawn in it is deleted a day after it was last used. See Privacy for what is kept.

Age

The games are for everyone, and the question packs are clean unless the host picks a Spicy pack, which is meant for adults. If you are under 18, play with your parent or guardian's OK.
